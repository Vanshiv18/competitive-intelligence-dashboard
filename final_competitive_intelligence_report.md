# COMPETITIVE INTELLIGENCE PROJECT
## Laptop Market Analysis and Competitive Benchmarking

### 1. Executive Summary

This project presents a competitive intelligence analysis of laptop
products across multiple brands and pricing segments.

The analysis covers 21 products across 5 brands.
It evaluates listed prices, product configurations, brand positioning,
market segments, and potential product opportunities.

The dashboard is designed to support structured competitive monitoring
and business decision-making.

### 2. Project Objectives

- Compare competitor product prices.
- Analyse brand-level positioning.
- Identify price and configuration differences.
- Classify products into market segments.
- Identify potential product opportunity gaps.
- Provide decision-support insights through an interactive dashboard.

### 3. Dataset Overview

Total products analysed: 21
Total brands analysed: 5
Lowest listed price: ₹39,990
Highest listed price: ₹202,990
Average listed price: ₹81,827

### 4. Methodology

1. Product information was compiled into a structured dataset.
2. Product records were organised by brand, model, price, RAM,
   storage, processor, and market segment.
3. Brand-wise and segment-wise aggregations were calculated.
4. Pricing comparisons were incorporated into the dashboard.
5. Potential NPI gaps were explored using product clustering.
6. Data-status fields were retained to distinguish source validation levels.

### 8. Strategic Recommendations

- Monitor competitor pricing and configuration changes regularly.
- Compare products using both price and technical specifications.
- Develop positioning for different customer segments.
- Validate product information before commercial decisions.
- Collect verified customer reviews for future sentiment analysis.
- Use product gaps as hypotheses for further market research.

### 9. Data Limitations

- The analysis is based on a 21-product sample.
- Some records require independent source validation.
- The dataset should not be interpreted as total market share.
- Verified customer review data is not currently available for sentiment
  conclusions.
- Pricing and specifications may change over time.

### 10. Conclusion

The project provides a structured competitive intelligence framework
for monitoring laptop products, comparing pricing, understanding
market segments, and identifying potential product opportunities.

The dashboard can be extended through regular data updates,
independent source validation, and verified customer feedback.
