4. KEY OBSERVATIONS
- The dataset covers multiple laptop price segments.
- Brand pricing varies across budget, mid-range, premium, and high-end categories.
- Product configuration should be considered alongside listed price.
- Missing ratings and reviews have not been treated as verified customer sentiment.
- Source validation status should be reviewed before making final business claims.

5. BUSINESS IMPLICATIONS
- Compare products using both price and configuration.
- Identify gaps where customer requirements are not represented by current products.
- Validate competitor pricing before making pricing decisions.
- Avoid treating this sample as total market share.

6. DATA LIMITATIONS
- The analysis is based on the available 21-product sample.
- Some records require independent source validation.
- Customer sentiment conclusions cannot be made without verified review data.
