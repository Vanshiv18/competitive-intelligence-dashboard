# Competitive Intelligence Dashboard

## Project Overview

An interactive Competitive Intelligence Dashboard designed to analyse
laptop products, compare competitor pricing, identify market segments,
and support strategic business decisions.

## Business Objective

To provide a structured framework for monitoring competitor products,
evaluating pricing differences, and identifying potential product
opportunities.

## Dataset

- Products analysed: 21
- Product category: Laptops
- Key fields: Brand, Model, Price, RAM, Storage, Processor, Segment
- Data validation status is maintained for source transparency.

## Technology Stack

- Python
- Pandas
- Streamlit
- Data Analysis
- Competitive Intelligence
- Business Analytics

## Dashboard Modules

1. KPI Overview
2. Pricing Benchmark
3. Sentiment & Positioning
4. Battle Cards
5. NPI Gaps
6. Market Insights

## Project Workflow

1. Define the business problem
2. Collect and structure competitor product data
3. Organise product specifications
4. Analyse pricing and market segments
5. Develop competitive insights
6. Identify potential NPI gaps
7. Build an interactive dashboard
8. Generate business recommendations

## Business Insights

The project supports:
- Competitor price comparison
- Brand-level benchmarking
- Market segment analysis
- Product configuration comparison
- Competitive monitoring
- Product opportunity identification

## Limitations

- The dataset represents a 21-product sample.
- Some product records require independent validation.
- The dataset does not represent total market share.
- Verified customer review data is not currently available.

## Future Scope

- Automated data collection
- Scheduled competitor price monitoring
- Verified customer review analysis
- Alert system for price changes
- Expanded product coverage
- Advanced predictive analytics
